What this lite version can and cannot do
  • It reads the page source and checks it. It does not render the page, click anything or call any API.
  • A browser page cannot fetch another website, so a URL cannot be loaded here. Open your page, view its source, and paste it above.
  • Results are static checks. Real click-through, tap-to-call and print-fit testing needs a browser runner such as Playwright.
  • APK files need an Android emulator on a server, so they are not part of this version.
