✏️ Check yourself

If it is 35 °C outside, should you wear a coat?

No. 35 °C is about 95 °F, a hot summer day.

You measure a foot at 27 cm. Which US men's size is closest?

US men's 9 (UK 8, EU 42.5).

It is 22:00 in London. Is it day or night in Tokyo?

Tokyo is 8 hours ahead in winter and 8 in summer: about 06:00 the next morning, so just after sunrise.

✏️ Check yourself

A recipe needs 3 tablespoons. How many teaspoons?

9 teaspoons. 1 tablespoon is 3 teaspoons.

Does a cup of flour weigh the same as a cup of sugar?

No. Flour is about 120 g per cup, sugar about 200 g. Denser things weigh more.

Your oven only shows °F and the recipe says 200 °C. What do you set?

About 400 °F (that is Gas Mark 6).

💡 Handy:1 US stick of butter = 8 tablespoons = ½ cup = about 113 g.
💡 Handy:1 litre is a little more than 4 cups (4.2).

✏️ Check yourself

A ladder is 5 m long and its foot is 3 m from a wall. How high does it reach?

4 m, since 3² + 4² = 5².

A £40 jacket is 30% off. What do you pay?

£28.

A circle has radius 5. What is its area?

π × 5² ≈ 78.5 square units.
