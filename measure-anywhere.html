Checking AR support…

  1. Move the phone slowly until a white ring appears on the floor or surface.
  2. Aim the ring at one end of the object and tap.
  3. Aim at the other end and tap. The length shows on screen and is saved below.

Good light and textured surfaces help. Typical accuracy is 1-3 cm, so use a tape for anything critical.
