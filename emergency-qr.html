How Privacy Is Protected
01
QR holds masked number
The code only contains your Google Voice or other forwarding number — never your real one.
02
Caller sees masked number
When scanned, the caller's phone dials the forwarding number. Your real number is invisible to them.
03
Call routes to you
The forwarding service silently redirects the call to your actual phone in real time.
04
Print & place anywhere
Attach to your bag, car, ID card, or door — anyone can scan in an emergency without knowing who you are.
